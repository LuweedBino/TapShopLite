# AGENTS.md

TapShopLite is the standalone version of the TapShop Video Studio (from github.com/LuweedBino/tapshop, `vendor/tapshop-studio/`): the video editor with its two story makers, the **B-roll builder** (`video-broll.js`) and the **Review showcase** (`video-reviews.js`). There's no account, no server and no Media Vault: files come from the device and go back to it.

- It's a static site with no build step. Vercel serves the folder as is. `index.html` is the home page (what TapShopLite is, free during early access, sign-up); `studio.html` is the editor (TapShop's `video.html`; `/studio` rewrites to it in `vercel.json`).
- **Sign-in** (Supabase, no passwords): `config.js` holds `enabled`, the project URL, publishable key and the extra provider buttons; `auth.js` loads supabase-js v2 from jsDelivr on demand and exposes `window.TapAuth` (`on`, `session()`, `email(address)` = magic link, `provider(name)`, `signOut()`, `onChange(fn)`; implicit flow so email links work in another browser; both redirect to `studio.html`). Sign-up is required: `studio.html`'s head hides the page and sends visitors without a session to `./?signin=1#join`. With `enabled: false` or no URL/key, sign-in is off and everything is open. The start screen shows a 👤 account link (`#vsAcct`) when sign-in is on. Setup steps for the owner: `SIGN-IN-SETUP.md`. `privacy.html` and `terms.html` (linked from the home page footer) are the pages Google's sign-in branding asks for; keep them true to what the site does.
- The code is classic scripts, not modules. Keep the script order at the end of `index.html`: text-catalog → studio-catalog → video → panel-layout → timeline-strips → color-picker → video-start → video-camera → video-gfx → video-gfx-more → video-reviews → video-broll → video-captions → video-autosave → video-export → video-toolbar → video-topbar, then the `tapshop-theme` CSS and JS.
- The Vault, "Sell this" and dashboard messages switch themselves off outside the TapShop iframe (`inTapShop()` in `video.js`). `vault-picker.js` isn't included; anything that uses `VaultPicker` must check `typeof VaultPicker !== 'undefined'` first.
- The start screen's two maker banners sit in `#vsQuick` (built in `video-broll.js`) under a **Quick Video Makers** heading (`.vs-qh`), right below the three-paths intro, inside a lasting pink border (stronger in dark mode). Steps 1–3 sit below it in `#vsSteps`, a **1-2-3 Steps** section with the same border.
- The start screen's top right has a light/dark switch (`#vsTheme`, calls `theme()` and follows `body.light`, so it stays in step with the editor's `#themeButton`); on phones it shows only the icon and the switch.
- **Free music** in the B-roll builder is made in the browser (`makeBeat`, no audio files or licences): 23 styles in `MOODS`, each a few settings (chords, drum steps, optional `bass`, `bassPat`, `lead`, `clap`, `rim`, `roll`, `drums`, `padType`, `padVol`, `chordBars`, `boom`; missing = the original sound). Keep new styles at about the same loudness (RMS ≈ 0.1–0.15). **▶ Listen** renders 10 seconds of the chosen one.
- **Captions and tidying in the b-roll builder**: two boxes, **✨ Add captions when someone talks** (`tapshop-broll-captions`) and **✂️ Cut pauses and “um”s** (`tapshop-broll-tidy`), both on unless turned off. While building, clips that talk (or every clip after Keep my order) go through `window.veClipWords({assetId,in,out})` (`video-captions.js`, the captions engine; words in the file's own seconds, `.est` when only sentence timing came back). Talking clips are trimmed to their first and last word, `tidy()` splits them around pauses over 0.5 s and um/uh (`FILLER`), and the same words become the Captions track through `window.vePlaceCaptions(words)` (last Captions style, else Pop). Fillers are left out of captions when tidying. If the engine can't load, the video builds as before and says so. `window.veAutoCaptions(say)` captions the whole timeline without the dialog.
- **Smarter b-roll** (`video-broll.js`): **✨ Pick for me** is the default story tile and music choice (`autoStory`, `mood === 'auto'`). After ✨ Sort my clips, `choose(true)` picks the story whose parts fit the clips best (`bestStory`: picture + word fit, all parts used, not more parts than clips) and the music from what the clips mostly show (`bestMood`, `LOOK_MOOD`; talky videos get calmer beats). Without the clip reader it goes by clip count (`BY_COUNT`) and the story's own music. Step 3 says what was picked and has a Music menu. Words on screen come from what talking clips say (`suggest`/`partText`: a sentence with the part's usual words, any sentence for the hook, fillers stripped, shortened, plus the template's emoji); typed words win. Before cutting, `scan()` scores up to 16 tiny frames per non-talking clip (sharpness, brightness, jumpiness) and `cut()` uses the best-scoring stretch (`bestStart`). **🎬 Add movement on the beat** (`tapshop-broll-motion`, on by default) sets clip `motion`, `intro` and `pulse`.
- **Clip movement** (`video.js`, `clipMotion`): optional clip fields `motion` (zoomIn, zoomOut, panLeft/Right/Up/Down, drift, close), `intro` (punch, flash, whip; the first moments of the clip) and `pulse` (seconds between small beat bumps, timed from the start of the video). Drawn in `draw()` from the playhead, so the preview and both exports match; missing fields = still. The clip inspector has Movement and Start of clip menus.
- The first time the editor shows (start screen closed), the timeline toolbar glides to its far end and back (`video-toolbar.js`, scroll-snap off during the glide). Skipped when it all fits, for reduced motion, and stopped by any touch or wheel on the bar.
- Auto-save keeps the 12 most recent videos in IndexedDB `tapshop-video-local` (the start screen's Recent videos).
- AI captions and the B-roll builder's clip sorting load transformers.js from jsDelivr and models from Hugging Face in the visitor's browser, so don't add a Content-Security-Policy that blocks those.
- Chrome on Android lets idle videos "sleep" (`readyState` < 2, so `draw()` skipped them and the preview went black). `draw()` calls `wakeVideo(v, t)` for a sleeping video while paused: a short muted `play()`, then back to the frame and a redraw (`v._waking` guards it).
- Anything drawn from a `<video>` goes through `paintVideo`, and anything that changes the sound must be added to `mixSound` (`video-export.js`), so preview and export match.
- **Save video** (top bar) opens the export: an MP4 with H.264 picture and AAC sound at 30 fps, short side = the chosen resolution (`exportSize()`, so 1080p vertical is 1080×1920), the format TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube all accept. The frame-by-frame path (`video-export.js`) only uses `avc` + `aac`; otherwise the real-time recorder runs and, if its file isn't H.264 + AAC MP4 (`socialReady()`), says some apps may refuse it. There are no project save or open buttons; auto-save keeps Recent videos.
- Mobile first: check every change at phone width (about 390 px) as well as on a desktop.
- Font and Mediabunny licences live in `licenses/`; keep them when adding fonts or libraries.
- Work on a branch and open a PR into `main`. Commit messages are short, plain English, and describe what the user gets.
