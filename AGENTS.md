# AGENTS.md

TapShopLite is the standalone version of the TapShop Video Studio (from github.com/LuweedBino/tapshop, `vendor/tapshop-studio/`): the video editor with its two story makers, the **B-roll Video Maker** (`video-broll.js`, called the b-roll builder in older notes below; toolbar button **🎞️ B-roll Video**) and the **Reviews Video Maker** (`video-reviews.js`, once the Review showcase; toolbar button **⭐ Reviews Video**). `video-toolbar.js` and `video-broll.js` find these buttons by `/Review/`. There's no Media Vault: files come from the device and go back to it. The only server code is in `api/`: the optional cloud AI and account deletion.

- It's a static site with no build step. Vercel serves the folder as is, at **https://tapshoplite.app** (bought through Vercel; www and tapshoplite.vercel.app also work; `index.html` has the canonical link). `index.html` is the home page (what TapShopLite is, free during early access, sign-up; a light/dark switch `#mode` in the top bar saves `forma-theme`, the same setting the video maker uses; **See it in action** shows three looping pure-CSS cartoon demos in mini phones (`.demos`: a real talking clip, `media/demo-talking.mp4` from Coverr, with captions, hook and "Link in bio" timed to the video's own clock in the home page script and playing only while on screen; the b-roll builder turning raw clips into a finished video, and the review showcase: three review screenshot cards animating in, a rating, then "Link in bio" with confetti; each has a tick list of what it does; a fourth, **Full video editor**, pans slowly over `media/editor-layers.jpg`, a real phone screenshot of the editor with seven layers), stacked one under another and centred on every screen size, still for reduced motion); `studio.html` is the editor (TapShop's `video.html`; `/studio` rewrites to it in `vercel.json`).
- **Sign-in** (Supabase, email + password like TapShop; emails are sent from hello@tapshoplite.app through Resend as Supabase's custom SMTP, set up by the owner per `EMAIL-SETUP.md`, with branded templates in `email-templates/`; "Confirm email" may be on or off and the site handles both): `config.js` holds `enabled`, the project URL, publishable key and the extra provider buttons; `auth.js` loads supabase-js v2 from jsDelivr on demand and exposes `window.TapAuth` (`on`, `session()`, `signUp(email, password)`, `signIn(email, password)`, `resend(email)`, `resetPassword(email)` (link to `reset.html`, which sets a new password with `updatePassword(password)`), `email(address)` = magic link (unused), `provider(name)`, `signOut()`, `onChange(fn)`; implicit flow so email links work in another browser; both redirect to `studio.html`). Sign-up is required: `studio.html`'s head hides the page and sends visitors without a session to `./?signin=1#join`. With `enabled: false` or no URL/key, sign-in is off and everything is open. The home page's sign-in form has **Forgot your password?** (sign-in mode), a **Check your email** panel with **Send it again** (after a sign-up that needs confirming, or a reset), and the signed-in card has **Change my password** (emails a reset link). `./?login=1#join` opens it in sign-in mode. When sign-in is on, `account-menu.js` (loaded after `video-topbar.js`) turns the start screen's 👤 (`#vsAcct`) and a matching button in the editor's top bar (`#veAcct`, before the light/dark switch) into an account menu: name/email, **My account**, **Invite a friend** (`veInvite()`: share sheet, else copy link), Home page, Sign out. **`account.html`** (My account) edits the name (`user_metadata.name`), email (`changeEmail`, confirmed by email; paste `email-templates/change-email.html` as Supabase's Change email template), password, shows cloud AI videos made today (`app_metadata.ai`), invite (share, copy, email), sign out / sign out on all devices, and **Delete my account** (type DELETE; `api/delete-account.js` checks the user's token and deletes that user with `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`). TapAuth also has `user()`, `updateProfile(data)`, `changeEmail(email)`, `signOut(scope)`, `deleteAccount()`. Setup steps for the owner: `SIGN-IN-SETUP.md`. `privacy.html` and `terms.html` (linked from the home page footer) are the pages Google's sign-in branding asks for; keep them true to what the site does.
- The code is classic scripts, not modules. Keep the script order at the end of `index.html`: text-catalog → studio-catalog → video → panel-layout → timeline-strips → color-picker → video-start → video-camera → video-gfx → video-gfx-more → video-frame → video-reviews → video-broll → video-captions → video-autosave → video-export → video-cleansound → video-toolbar → video-topbar → account-menu, then the `tapshop-theme` CSS and JS.
- The Vault, "Sell this" and dashboard messages switch themselves off outside the TapShop iframe (`inTapShop()` in `video.js`). `vault-picker.js` isn't included; anything that uses `VaultPicker` must check `typeof VaultPicker !== 'undefined'` first.
- The start screen's two maker banners sit in `#vsQuick` (built in `video-broll.js`) under a **Quick Video Makers** heading (`.vs-qh`), right below the three-paths intro, inside a lasting pink border (stronger in dark mode). Steps 1–3 sit below it in `#vsSteps`, a **1-2-3 Steps** section with the same border.
- Every start-screen section has the same pink border: the three paths (`.vs-lead`), Quick Video Makers, 1-2-3 Steps, Recent videos (`#vsRecentSec`) and **Quick tips** (`.vs-tipsec`). Quick tips (`TIPS` in `video-start.js`, about 50: one per feature of the site plus pointers for better videos) sit in one sideways strip (`#vsTips`) that scrolls by itself at about 24 px/s, looping through a copy of the cards; touch, hover, wheel or focus pause it for 4 s; off for reduced motion. Keep tips true when features change. There's no sample video button any more (`loadDemo` still exists).
- The start screen's top right has a light/dark switch (`#vsTheme`, calls `theme()` and follows `body.light`, so it stays in step with the editor's `#themeButton`); on phones it shows only the icon and the switch.
- **Free music** is made in the browser (`makeBeat` in `video-broll.js`, no audio files or licences): 28 styles in `MOODS`, each a few settings (chords, drum steps, optional `bass`, `bassPat`, `lead` triangle|square|sine|bell|piano|guitar (Karplus-Strong string), `clap`, `rim`, `roll`, `drums`, `padType`, `padVol`, `chordBars`, `boom`; missing = the original sound), and each has its own tune in `MEL`: two one-bar phrases played A A B A from the second bar, written as chord-note numbers (0 = root, `-` hold, `.` rest) so they fit any chords; the arpeggio plays softer under the tune. Keep new styles at about the same loudness (RMS ≈ 0.1–0.16). **▶ Listen** renders 10 seconds. Besides the B-roll builder, every audio track has a **🎵 Free music** button (`openFreeMusic(trackId)`: listen, then **Use** makes it as long as the video, 15 s–5 min); the toolbar's **🎵 Audio** opens the new track with its buttons showing. `window.veMakeBeat` is exposed for tests.
- **Captions and tidying in the b-roll builder**: two boxes, **✨ Add captions when someone talks** (`tapshop-broll-captions`) and **✂️ Cut pauses and “um”s** (`tapshop-broll-tidy`), both on unless turned off. While building, clips that talk (or every clip after Keep my order) go through `window.veClipWords({assetId,in,out})` (`video-captions.js`, the captions engine; words in the file's own seconds, `.est` when only sentence timing came back). Talking clips are trimmed to their first and last word, `tidy()` splits them around pauses over 0.5 s and um/uh (`FILLER`), and the same words become the Captions track through `window.vePlaceCaptions(words)` (last Captions style, else Pop). Fillers are left out of captions when tidying. If the engine can't load, the video builds as before and says so. `window.veAutoCaptions(say)` captions the whole timeline without the dialog.
- **Cloud AI** (optional, Groq): `api/words.js` is a Vercel function (the only server code) that keeps `GROQ_API_KEY` secret. It checks the Supabase session, counts one use per build per person per day in the user's `app_metadata.ai` (needs `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`; `AI_PER_DAY`, default 10), sends WAV sound to Whisper (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`, with an "um"-filled prompt so fillers are kept), and on a second JSON call asks `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` for fumbles and retakes (never more than half the words; also whole throwaway takes). In the app, `window.veCloudWords(list)` (`video-captions.js`) joins the talking clips' sound into ≤2-minute WAVs (1 s silence between clips; Vercel's body limit is 4.5 MB) and returns words per clip with `drop` on mistakes. The B-roll builder uses it first when the **☁️ Use cloud AI** box (`tapshop-broll-cloud`) is ticked, then falls back to `veClipWords` on any error; `tidy()` and captions treat `drop` words like fillers, and a clip whose words are all fillers, `drop` or `EMPTY` words ("ok", "alright"…) is left out of the video (`junk`). Switch: `window.TAPSHOP_AI.cloud` in `config.js`. Owner setup: `AI-SETUP.md`. `privacy.html` and `terms.html` describe it; keep them true.
- **Clean sound** (`video-cleansound.js`, all on the device): `veCleanSound(asset)` decodes the video's sound at 48 kHz, finds record start/stop clicks in the first and last 0.8 s on the original (5 ms bursts 6× louder than around them; muted with soft edges, plus 80 ms fades at both ends), removes steady noise by spectral subtraction against a noise print (15th percentile per frequency; floor 0.12, oversubtraction 2.2, rumble under 70 Hz cut), and keeps the result as `a.cleanFile` (WAV). The preview plays it through a hidden `<audio>` (`a.cleanEl`) that follows the video element into `a.gain` (the video's own `a.node` goes to a zero-volume gain `a.silent` instead; it must stay connected to the speakers, because Chrome times the picture by its sound and a disconnected one made the preview stutter and flash); `mixSound` uses `cleanFile` for the export. Clips get `c.clean`, and auto-save's reopen calls `veRecleanSound()`. `veOriginalSound(asset)` undoes it. Ways in: the B-roll builder's **🔇 Clean up the sound** box (`tapshop-broll-clean`, on by default) and a Smart tools card (every clip, or back to the original).
- **Smarter b-roll** (`video-broll.js`): **✨ Pick for me** is the default story tile and music choice (`autoStory`, `mood === 'auto'`). After ✨ Sort my clips, `choose(true)` picks the story whose parts fit the clips best (`bestStory`: picture + word fit, all parts used, not more parts than clips) and the music from what the clips mostly show (`bestMood`, `LOOK_MOOD`; talky videos get calmer beats). Without the clip reader it goes by clip count (`BY_COUNT`) and the story's own music. Step 3 says what was picked and has a Music menu. Words on screen come from what talking clips say (`suggest`/`partText`: a sentence with the part's usual words, any sentence for the hook, fillers stripped, shortened, plus the template's emoji); typed words win. Before cutting, `scan()` scores up to 16 tiny frames per non-talking clip (sharpness, brightness, jumpiness) and `cut()` uses the best-scoring stretch (`bestStart`). **🎬 Add slow, smooth zooms** (`tapshop-broll-motion`, on by default) gives each non-talking clip a very slow zoom, alternating `slowIn`/`slowOut` (6%, steady speed), and talking jump cuts alternate with `closeSoft` (a slight 7% close-up). No `intro` or `pulse`: the busier mix of pans, beat bumps and punch/flash/whip felt clunky.
- **Clip movement** (`video.js`, `clipMotion`): optional clip fields `motion` (zoomIn, zoomOut, panLeft/Right/Up/Down, drift, close, and the calm slowIn, slowOut, closeSoft), `intro` (punch, flash, whip; the first moments of the clip) and `pulse` (seconds between small beat bumps, timed from the start of the video). Drawn in `draw()` from the playhead, so the preview and both exports match; missing fields = still. The clip inspector has Movement and Start of clip menus.
- **Resize videos on the preview** (`video-frame.js`, after `video-gfx-more.js` so text and shapes get the tap first): tapping a video on the preview picks it (topmost visible video layer, else the main clip under the playhead; also picks its strip) and draws a dashed pink box with four corner handles (kept inside the frame). Drag inside moves it, a corner scales it about its centre keeping its shape, a double-tap resets it. Main clips get optional `zoom` (×, 0.2–5), `px`, `py` (shift as a share of the frame width/height), folded into `clipMotion` so preview and both exports match; video layers use their own `x`, `y`, `scale`. Undo-able (snapshot on the first move). The box is never drawn while exporting.
- **Strips: tap, drag, double-tap** (end of `timeline-strips.js`): one tap picks a strip (`pickedStrip`, pink `.picked` outline; Delete/Split/Duplicate act on it) and keeps its editing menu closed so it can be dragged; a double-tap (second tap within 0.4 s, `tapStrip`) or Enter opens/closes the menu (`expandedStrip`). Dragging, resizing, nudging, duplicating and Undo never open a menu. A tap that lands on the ✂ split button within 0.45 s of the first tap counts as the double-tap (it used to split the strip). Text, stickers, effects, captions, video layers and audio can be dragged up or down (more than 14 px): over the middle of a row of the same `type` it joins that row (`lane`, highlighted `.drop-into`), between rows / above / below it gets a new layer (`lane = uid()`, pink "New layer" line), moved in the array next to its new row (array order = layer order). Rows exist only for their strips, so a row left empty goes away. A pink note at the top of the screen, away from the timeline (`stripTip`), explains this for 9 s the first 3 times the editor is used, only once the editor is showing with strips on the timeline (`editorWithStrips()`: page loaded, start screen closed; it goes away if the start screen opens) (✕ ends it); there's also a Quick tip.
- **Main clips ↔ video layers** (`clipToLayer`/`layerToClip`, end of `timeline-strips.js`): a main video clip dragged up or down off the main row becomes a video layer (picture in picture, `scale` 100 so it looks the same) at the same time and part of the file (`mainDrop`: over the middle of a video-layer row it joins that row, else a new layer, line labelled "New video layer"); a video layer dropped on the main row goes back into the main video at its time (or the first free spot after it). Other main clips keep their places (offsets fixed first), so a gap is left for its 🗑. The strip note's counter key is `tapshop-strip-tip2` (reset once because it had been used up on the start screen).
- **🗑 Empty space** (`addGapBins`/`closeGap`, end of `timeline-strips.js`): every row shows a small dashed 🗑 in each gap of 0.15 s or more between two of its strips. On the main video row it deletes that time from the whole video (`rippleGap(A, d, true)`: everything after moves left; with `keep`, things inside the gap move to its start instead of being removed); on other rows only that row's later strips move. Dragging a strip over the middle of a row of another kind makes a new track above or below it (by which half).
- **Ripple delete** (end of `timeline-strips.js`, `rippleGap(A, d)`): deleting a main-row clip closes the gap and moves everything on the other rows with it: later main clips with their own `offset`, graphic tracks (text, stickers, effects; captions also drop and shift their `words`), and extra tracks (video layers, audio, music via `start`/`end`/`speed`). Items only on the deleted clip are removed, items crossing it get shorter. When there are captions, the toast points to **🔄 Redo captions** (`capRedo(id)` in `video-captions.js`, two `.cap-redo` buttons in the Captions strip: one at the top next to ★ Save my style and Delete captions, one in the Timing row): it re-listens to the timeline as it is now (cloud AI first if on and not turned off in the B-roll builder, else `listenAll` on the device), drops um/uh, and replaces the Captions track keeping its style; Undo-able. The snapshot taken before the delete covers it all, so one Undo restores everything.
- The first time the editor shows (start screen closed), the timeline toolbar glides to its far end and back (`video-toolbar.js`, scroll-snap off during the glide). Skipped when it all fits, for reduced motion, and stopped by any touch or wheel on the bar.
- Auto-save keeps the 12 most recent videos in IndexedDB `tapshop-video-local` (the start screen's Recent videos).
- AI captions and the B-roll builder's clip sorting load transformers.js from jsDelivr and models from Hugging Face in the visitor's browser, so don't add a Content-Security-Policy that blocks those.
- Chrome on Android lets idle videos "sleep" (`readyState` < 2, so `draw()` skipped them and the preview went black). `draw()` calls `wakeVideo(v, t)` for a sleeping video while paused: a short muted `play()`, then back to the frame and a redraw (`v._waking` guards it).
- Anything drawn from a `<video>` goes through `paintVideo`, and anything that changes the sound must be added to `mixSound` (`video-export.js`), so preview and export match.
- **Save video** (top bar) opens the export: an MP4 with H.264 picture and AAC sound at 30 fps, short side = the chosen resolution (`exportSize()`, so 1080p vertical is 1080×1920), the format TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube all accept. The frame-by-frame path (`video-export.js`) only uses `avc` + `aac`; otherwise the real-time recorder runs and, if its file isn't H.264 + AAC MP4 (`socialReady()`), says some apps may refuse it. There are no project save or open buttons; auto-save keeps Recent videos.
- Mobile first: check every change at phone width (about 390 px) as well as on a desktop.
- Font and Mediabunny licences live in `licenses/`; keep them when adding fonts or libraries.
- Work on a branch and open a PR into `main`. Commit messages are short, plain English, and describe what the user gets.
