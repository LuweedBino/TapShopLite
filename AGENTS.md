# AGENTS.md

TapShopLite is the standalone version of the TapShop Video Studio (from github.com/LuweedBino/tapshop, `vendor/tapshop-studio/`): the video editor with its two story makers, the **B-roll Video Maker** (`video-broll.js`, called the b-roll builder in older notes below; toolbar button **🎞️ B-roll Video**) and the **Reviews Video Maker** (`video-reviews.js`, once the Review showcase; toolbar button **⭐ Reviews Video**). `video-toolbar.js` and `video-broll.js` find these buttons by `/Review/`. There's no Media Vault: files come from the device and go back to it. The only server code is in `api/`: the optional cloud AI and account deletion.

- It's a static site with no build step. Vercel serves the folder as is, at **https://tapshoplite.app** (bought through Vercel; www and tapshoplite.vercel.app also work; `index.html` has the canonical link). `index.html` is the home page (what TapShopLite is, free during early access, sign-up; a light/dark switch `#mode` in the top bar saves `forma-theme`, the same setting the video maker uses; **See it in action** shows three looping pure-CSS cartoon demos in mini phones (`.demos`: a real talking clip, `media/demo-talking.mp4` from Coverr, with captions, hook and "Link in bio" timed to the video's own clock in the home page script and playing only while on screen; the b-roll builder turning raw clips into a finished video, and the review showcase: three review screenshot cards animating in, a rating, then "Link in bio" with confetti; each has a tick list of what it does; a fourth, **Full video editor**, pans slowly over `media/editor-layers.jpg`, a real phone screenshot of the editor with seven layers), stacked one under another and centred on every screen size, still for reduced motion); `studio.html` is the editor (TapShop's `video.html`; `/studio` rewrites to it in `vercel.json`).
- **Sign-in** (Supabase, email + password like TapShop; emails are sent from hello@tapshoplite.app through Resend as Supabase's custom SMTP, set up by the owner per `EMAIL-SETUP.md`, with branded templates in `email-templates/`; "Confirm email" may be on or off and the site handles both): `config.js` holds `enabled`, the project URL, publishable key and the extra provider buttons; `auth.js` loads supabase-js v2 from jsDelivr on demand and exposes `window.TapAuth` (`on`, `session()`, `signUp(email, password)`, `signIn(email, password)`, `resend(email)`, `resetPassword(email)` (link to `reset.html`, which sets a new password with `updatePassword(password)`), `email(address)` = magic link (unused), `provider(name)`, `signOut()`, `onChange(fn)`; implicit flow so email links work in another browser; both redirect to `studio.html`). Sign-up is required: `studio.html`'s head hides the page and sends visitors without a session to `./?signin=1#join`. With `enabled: false` or no URL/key, sign-in is off and everything is open. The home page's sign-in form has **Forgot your password?** (sign-in mode), a **Check your email** panel with **Send it again** (after a sign-up that needs confirming, or a reset), and the signed-in card has **Change my password** (emails a reset link). `./?login=1#join` opens it in sign-in mode. When sign-in is on, `account-menu.js` (loaded after `video-topbar.js`) turns the start screen's 👤 (`#vsAcct`) and a matching button in the editor's top bar (`#veAcct`, before the light/dark switch) into an account menu: name/email, **My account**, **Invite a friend** (`veInvite()`: share sheet, else copy link), Home page, Sign out. The menu closes on an outside tap, Escape, or when its button actually moves (scroll/resize), not on every scroll event: the self-scrolling Quick tips strip and toolbar glide fire them constantly on phones and used to close it instantly. **`account.html`** (My account) edits the name (`user_metadata.name`), email (`changeEmail`, confirmed by email; paste `email-templates/change-email.html` as Supabase's Change email template), password, shows cloud AI videos made today (`app_metadata.ai`), invite (share, copy, email), sign out / sign out on all devices, and **Delete my account** (type DELETE; `api/delete-account.js` checks the user's token and deletes that user with `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`). TapAuth also has `user()`, `updateProfile(data)`, `changeEmail(email)`, `signOut(scope)`, `deleteAccount()`. Setup steps for the owner: `SIGN-IN-SETUP.md`. `privacy.html` and `terms.html` (linked from the home page footer) are the pages Google's sign-in branding asks for; keep them true to what the site does.

- **Free music** is made in the browser (`makeBeat` in `video-broll.js`, no audio files or licences): 78 styles in `MOODS`, each a few settings (chords, drum steps, optional `bass`, `bassPat`, `lead` triangle|square|sine|bell|piano|guitar (Karplus-Strong string), `clap`, `rim`, `roll`, `drums`, `padType`, `padVol`, `chordBars`, `boom`; missing = the original sound), and each has its own tune in `MEL`: two one-bar phrases played A A B A from the second bar, written as chord-note numbers (0 = root, `-` hold, `.` rest) so they fit any chords; the arpeggio plays softer under the tune. Keep new styles at about the same loudness (RMS ≈ 0.1–0.16). **▶ Listen** renders 10 seconds. Besides the B-roll builder, every audio track has a **🎵 Free music** button (`openFreeMusic(trackId)`: listen, then **Use** makes it as long as the video, 15 s–5 min); the toolbar's **🎵 Audio** opens the new track with its buttons showing. `window.veMakeBeat` is exposed for tests. **For creators** (`CREATOR`: hook, unbox, tech, flash, luxe, reveal, haul, story, fitness, tutorial, each with a `for` line) come first in every music menu; each has its own key, chords, tempo, bassline (`bassNotes`) and tune, and optional `fx` on the whole mix: `mid` (a dip around 1.8 kHz so a voice sits on top; all ten use it), `noFade`, `comp:'tight'`, `lp`, `bright`, `padHP`, `padAtt`, `chorus`, `wobble`, `crackle`, `reverb`, dotted `delay`, `pump` (music dips on each kick; drums bypass it), `rise`, `pan`, `wide`, `sat` (bass drive), and `split` (Before & after: up to 4 quiet filtered bars without drums or bass, then the filter opens at the drop). New leads marimba, kalimba, chime, rhodes (FM), pluck; `stab`, `tick`, `shaker`, `arpPat`, `melOrder`, `melOct`, `fromStart`, `vol`. Styles without these fields sound exactly as before. **Lifestyle, home, fashion & beauty** (`LIFE`, batch 1: desk, rainy, kitchen, tidy, pet, toy, coffee, plant, scrub, apartment, sneaker, thrift, jewelry, grwm, silk) is the second music group (`GROUPS()`, also `window.veMusicGroups`, used by every music menu and the Reviews maker); each has its own key/mode, chords, tempo, bassline and tune, and `fx.mid`. They add `melLead`/`stabLead` (a different sound for the tune or stabs; `pluck(t, m, pan, amp, d, L)` takes the instrument), leads `felt`, `glock`, `pizz` (slides up into each note), `bow` (string pad), `snap`, and `fx.hp`, `warm`, `tape`, `swell`, `autopan`, `metal` (808 hats), `short`, `delay.steps`. `LOOK_MOOD` sends food to kitchen, clean to tidy, outside to plant. **Style, tech, gaming & productivity** (`TECH`, batch 2: dupes, capsule, perfume, athleisure, shades, rgb, saas, notion, droptest, smarthome, wfh, filmmaker, subbox, hustle, scam) is the third group. They add `resp`/`respLead` (an answering tune, panned opposite the main one by `fx.call`; set after `MEL` for dupes), `build` (ticks speed up for three bars, then an impact bar with crash and the beat), `layers` ({bass, drums, stab, arp, mel}: the bar each joins), lead `glass` (FM), `fx.lfoLp` {lo, hi, rate}, `fx.bassBP`, `reverb.gate`, `delay.ping`. **Sales, reviews & storytelling** (`STORY`, batch 3: deinfluence, viral, giftguide, smallbiz, testimonial, travel, car, booktok, mistakes, finale) is the fourth group; they add leads `quirk` (bends into each note and wobbles), `musicbox`, `stacc` (short strings), `fx.center` (melodic parts in the middle) and `ending` + `endChord` (+ `endLead`): nothing new starts in the last 2.6 s except one final chord, a crash and a kick, which ring out. Mood keys must not reuse a story template id (hence `deinfluence`, not `honest`). No two of batches 1–3 share a tempo, and no tune phrase is reused anywhere. **`window.AUDIO_LIBRARY`** (end of `video-sfx.js`) lists every music style and sound effect with `id`, `title`, `type` ('music'|'sfx'), `category`, `moods`, `useCases`, `keywords`, `bpm`, `durationSeconds` (music: default length, `anyLength`), `voiceoverSafe`, `play(seconds)` → `{stop()}` and `file()`; batches 1–3 have hand-written details (plus `mode` and `batch`), the others are filled from their style. Older styles get moods and uses from `OLD` in `video-sfx.js`. **Search and discovery** (`audio-search.js`, loaded after `video-sfx.js`): `searchAudio(query, {type, filter, pace, voiceoverSafe, favorites, recent, minDuration, maxDuration, limit})` splits the query into words and scores each across fields (title 10, useCases 6, moods 5, keywords 3, category 3; ×1 for a word or its start, ×0.8 inside a word, ×0.7 for a typo: one for 5+ letters, two for 8+; +5 when every word matched); results carry `_score`. `getQuickFilters()` (all music, sound effects, voiceover safe, 2-second hooks, aesthetic & cozy, high energy & sales, cinematic & story; each with `test` and `count`), `getPaceFilters()` (slow < 90, medium 90–120, fast > 120 BPM), `getSimilarTracks(id, limit = 4)` (shared moods ×3, shared use-case words ×2, tempo within 15 BPM, same category; same type only), and favorites / recent on this device (`toggleFavorite`, `getFavorites`, `isFavorite`, `recordRecent`, `getRecent`; keys `tapshop-audio-favs`, `tapshop-audio-recent`, 12 recent). `AUDIO_LIBRARY_SEARCH` now calls `searchAudio`. The **🎵 Free music** dialog has a search box, a pace menu and filter pills (plus ★ Favorites and 🕘 Recent); each row has ☆ (favorite) and ≈ (similar vibes). The pill row scrolls sideways by touch, mouse wheel (vertical turns into sideways) or mouse drag (a drag never picks a pill), with a thin pink scrollbar and a fade on the right edge until the end. With nothing set it shows the groups as before. **Use** and `TapSFX.add` record recent use.
- **Auto-ducking and fades** (preview and export match): `veTrackGain(t, T)` in `video.js` is the volume shape of an extra track at timeline time T: linear `fadeIn`/`fadeOut` (seconds) times, for audio tracks with `duck`, `1 − duckAmt × depth` (`veDuckDepth`: full inside a speech window, a 0.12 s ramp down before it and 0.3 s back up after). `syncExtra()` applies it every frame; `mixSound()` (`video-export.js`) writes it as a 200-points-per-second `setValueCurveAtTime` curve through `addSound(…, shape)`, after `await veSpeechReady()`. `mixSound` also now places main clips at `startOf(i)`, so gaps between clips stay silent as in the preview (they used to be packed together). **Speech windows** (end of `video-captions.js`): `veSpeechWindows()` (cached, recomputed after any `decorateStrips`) and `veSpeechReady()` (waits for all files): caption words (`offset + w.s/e`) where captions exist, else loudness per 50 ms (decoded at 16 kHz, threshold per file = max(15th percentile × 2.5, 95th × 0.18, 0.008)) of main clips, video layers and audio tracks that aren't music or effects (no `duck`, no `bpm`, no `sfxFor`, not `lane:'sfx'`); words under 0.4 s apart join, then 0.15 s lead and 0.35 s tail. New audio media gets `fadeIn` 0.3, `fadeOut` 1.5, `duck` on, `duckAmt` 0.7 (`loadTrackMedia`), except sound effects (`veIsSfx`: 0, 0, off); auto-save's reopen deletes those fields for older saved tracks so they sound as before. The audio track menu has Fade in, Fade out, a 🎙️ Auto-duck switch, Duck strength (0–100%) and the tempo. **Music track metadata**: `veMusicTrackMeta(key)` (`video-broll.js`: `bpm`, `fadeIn` 0 for `noFade` styles else 0.3, `fadeOut` 1.5, `duck`, `duckAmt`, `musicKey`) is set by 🎵 Free music's Use, the B-roll build (music volume 0.5 when talky, ducking does the rest) and the Reviews maker; they render with `makeBeat(…, {bare:1})` (no baked-in fades, only 5 ms against clicks) so the track's own fades apply, even after trimming. **Beat snapping** (`timeline-strips.js`): `beatTimes(skip)` lists every beat of each music track with a `bpm` (beat k at `offset + (k·60/bpm − start)/speed`, first beat at the file's start); `neighbourEdges()` adds them (leaving out a track's own beats while it moves), so `snapMove`/`snapEdge` snap drags and trims to beats within `SNAP` (Shift = free). `setRuler` is wrapped to draw `.beat-marks` (pink ticks every beat, taller every bar) on the ruler and `.beat-lines` (bar lines) on the music strip. Uploaded songs have no `bpm`, so no beat marks.
- **Sound effects** (`video-sfx.js`, `window.TapSFX`): 12 one-shot sounds made on the device (swoosh, pop, cash, shutter, sparkle, drop, click, buzz, ding, riser, scratch, whoosh; 0.05–1.2 s). Each is a function `(ctx, out, t)` in `FX` that builds its sound on any audio context and returns its length, with a level in `LEVEL` so they land about equally loud (peaks ≈ 0.6–0.9). `TapSFX.play(key)` plays one now (through the editor's limiter when its sound is running), `render`/`file` make a WAV, `add(key)` puts it on a new audio track at the playhead with `lane:'sfx'` (they share one row when they don't overlap; Undo-able). The **🔊 Sound FX** button sits after 🎵 Audio in the Add group (`video-toolbar.js` sorts it by its 🔊). **Safety limiter**: `veLimiter(ctx, to)` in `video.js` (compressor at −2 dB, ratio 20, 1 ms attack, then ×0.9) sits at the end of the preview (`veSpk` to the speakers, `veRec` to the recorder's stream) and the export mix (`off._out` in `mixSound`), so music, voices and effects landing together never clip. Anything new that makes sound connects to `veSpk`/`veRec`, not `audioCtx.destination`. **Sound on text and stickers**: text and shape strips get a **🔊 Sound effect** menu (`.sfx-pick`, added in a `renderGraphicTracks` wrapper, so it also shows in the preview pop-up) with ▶. `veSfxPick(id, key)` (Undo-able) replaces the strip's tied sound: an audio track with `sfxFor` = the strip id and `sfxKey`. `syncLinks()` runs before every `decorateStrips`: it keeps each tied sound at its strip's `offset` and deletes it when the strip is gone. The menu shows the tied track's `sfxKey`, so a duplicated strip starts with None. Tied sounds can start part-way into their strip (`sfxAt`, seconds; the menu only shows `sfxAt` 0). **The makers use them**: the B-roll builder's **🔊 Add sound effects** box (`tapshop-broll-sfx`, on by default) ties a sound to each line of words it places (`partSfx(part id, story)`: hook = bass drop on pas/versus/myth, record scratch on pov, else whoosh hit; problem/old/myth/bad = buzz; after = sparkle; solution/new/truth/good/result/proof/verdict/why/fav = ding; look/product = shutter; twist = scratch; cta = tap; before/night = swoosh; else pop). Its stories default to creator music (pas hook, beforeafter reveal, reasons haul, unboxing and dayinlife unbox, howto tutorial, myth tech, versus fitness, pov and honest story), `LOOK_MOOD` points at creator tracks, talky videos fall back to `story`, and `makeBeat(seconds, key, {dropAt})` puts the Before & after drop on the bar nearest the After part. The Reviews maker has **Sound effects** (whoosh on the opening line, a pop as each review lands, sparkle or tap on the ending) and **Music** (none by default; `window.veMoods`/`veCreatorMoods` from `video-broll.js`), all tied to the review video and remade on Update (`sounds(t)` in `video-reviews.js`). Effects render at least 0.15 s long, because `loadTrackMedia` refuses anything under 0.1 s.
- **Captions and tidying in the b-roll builder**: two boxes, **✨ Add captions when someone talks** (`tapshop-broll-captions`) and **✂️ Cut pauses and “um”s** (`tapshop-broll-tidy`), both on unless turned off. While building, clips that talk (or every clip after Keep my order) go through `window.veClipWords({assetId,in,out})` (`video-captions.js`, the captions engine; words in the file's own seconds, `.est` when only sentence timing came back). Talking clips are trimmed to their first and last word, `tidy()` splits them around pauses over 0.5 s and um/uh (`FILLER`), and the same words become the Captions track through `window.vePlaceCaptions(words)` (last Captions style, else Pop). Fillers are left out of captions when tidying. If the engine can't load, the video builds as before and says so. `window.veAutoCaptions(say)` captions the whole timeline without the dialog.
- **Cloud AI** (optional, Groq): `api/words.js` is a Vercel function (the only server code) that keeps `GROQ_API_KEY` secret. It checks the Supabase session, counts one use per build per person per day in the user's `app_metadata.ai` (needs `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY`; `AI_PER_DAY`, default 10), sends WAV sound to Whisper (`whisper-large-v3-turbo`, with an "um"-filled prompt so fillers are kept), and on a second JSON call asks `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` for fumbles and retakes (never more than half the words; also whole throwaway takes). In the app, `window.veCloudWords(list)` (`video-captions.js`) joins the talking clips' sound into ≤2-minute WAVs (1 s silence between clips; Vercel's body limit is 4.5 MB) and returns words per clip with `drop` on mistakes. The B-roll builder uses it first when the **☁️ Use cloud AI** box (`tapshop-broll-cloud`) is ticked, then falls back to `veClipWords` on any error; `tidy()` and captions treat `drop` words like fillers, and a clip whose words are all fillers, `drop` or `EMPTY` words ("ok", "alright"…) is left out of the video (`junk`). Switch: `window.TAPSHOP_AI.cloud` in `config.js`. Owner setup: `AI-SETUP.md`. `privacy.html` and `terms.html` describe it; keep them true.
- **Clean sound** (`video-cleansound.js`, all on the device): `veCleanSound(asset)` decodes the video's sound at 48 kHz, finds record start/stop clicks in the first and last 0.8 s on the original (5 ms bursts 6× louder than around them; muted with soft edges, plus 80 ms fades at both ends), removes steady noise by spectral subtraction against a noise print (15th percentile per frequency; floor 0.12, oversubtraction 2.2, rumble under 70 Hz cut), and keeps the result as `a.cleanFile` (WAV). The preview plays it through a hidden `<audio>` (`a.cleanEl`) that follows the video element into `a.gain` (the video's own `a.node` goes to a zero-volume gain `a.silent` instead; it must stay connected to the speakers, because Chrome times the picture by its sound and a disconnected one made the preview stutter and flash); `mixSound` uses `cleanFile` for the export. Clips get `c.clean`, and auto-save's reopen calls `veRecleanSound()`. `veOriginalSound(asset)` undoes it. Ways in: the B-roll builder's **🔇 Clean up the sound** box (`tapshop-broll-clean`, on by default) and a Smart tools card (every clip, or back to the original).
- **Smarter b-roll** (`video-broll.js`): **✨ Pick for me** is the default story tile and music choice (`autoStory`, `mood === 'auto'`). After ✨ Sort my clips, `choose(true)` picks the story whose parts fit the clips best (`bestStory`: picture + word fit, all parts used, not more parts than clips) and the music from what the clips mostly show (`bestMood`, `LOOK_MOOD`; talky videos get calmer beats). Without the clip reader it goes by clip count (`BY_COUNT`) and the story's own music. Step 3 says what was picked and has a Music menu. Words on screen come from what talking clips say (`suggest`/`partText`: a sentence with the part's usual words, any sentence for the hook, fillers stripped, shortened, plus the template's emoji); typed words win. Before cutting, `scan()` scores up to 16 tiny frames per non-talking clip (sharpness, brightness, jumpiness) and `cut()` uses the best-scoring stretch (`bestStart`). **🎬 Add slow, smooth zooms** (`tapshop-broll-motion`, on by default) gives each non-talking clip a very slow zoom, alternating `slowIn`/`slowOut` (6%, steady speed), and talking jump cuts alternate with `closeSoft` (a slight 7% close-up). No `intro` or `pulse`: the busier mix of pans, beat bumps and punch/flash/whip felt clunky.
- **Clip movement** (`video.js`, `clipMotion`): optional clip fields `motion` (zoomIn, zoomOut, panLeft/Right/Up/Down, drift, close, and the calm slowIn, slowOut, closeSoft), `intro` (punch, flash, whip; the first moments of the clip) and `pulse` (seconds between small beat bumps, timed from the start of the video). Drawn in `draw()` from the playhead, so the preview and both exports match; missing fields = still. The clip inspector has Movement and Start of clip menus.
- **Resize videos on the preview** (`video-frame.js`, after `video-gfx-more.js` so text and shapes get the tap first): tapping a video on the preview picks it (topmost visible video layer, else the main clip under the playhead; also picks its strip) and draws a dashed pink box with four corner handles (kept inside the frame). Drag inside moves it, a corner scales it about its centre keeping its shape; a double-tap opens its editing menu (which has ↺ Normal size). Main clips get optional `zoom` (×, 0.2–5), `px`, `py` (shift as a share of the frame width/height), folded into `clipMotion` so preview and both exports match; video layers use their own `x`, `y`, `scale`. Undo-able (snapshot on the first move). The box is never drawn while exporting.
- **Editing menu on the preview** (end of `video-frame.js`): a double-tap on text, a sticker or a video on the preview opens that strip's own editing menu in a pop-up (`.pv-pop`, bottom of the screen, pink border): the strip row's `.strip-options` block is moved into it, and moved again after every timeline re-render (`fillPop` in a `decorateStrips` wrapper), so every control works as on the timeline. Videos also get **↺ Normal size** (`veResetVideoFrame`). **✓ Apply**, ✕, Escape or a tap anywhere else (not the colour picker) closes it and redraws the timeline. In `video.js` the text/shape double-tap opens this menu (`veOpenPreviewEditor`) instead of the warp/point modes, which are buttons inside the menu. The preview hint reads "Drag to move · corners resize · double-tap to edit".
- **Big text** (`video.js`): text and shape `size` goes up to 3000 (corner drag, number boxes, insert dialog, `editGraphic`); `x`/`y` may be −100…200 % so things can sit partly off-frame. A text line is squeezed to `fitW(t, w)` = 94 % of the frame up to size 240 (as before), growing in step with the size above that, so big text runs past the edges smoothly and old videos look the same. Text animations `giantIn` ("Giant → normal", 6× → 1×) and `giantOut` ("Normal → giant") run over the strip's whole length, eased (`applyTextMotion`).
- **Strips: tap, drag, double-tap** (end of `timeline-strips.js`): one tap picks a strip (`pickedStrip`, pink `.picked` outline; Delete/Split/Duplicate act on it) and keeps its editing menu closed so it can be dragged; a double-tap (second tap within 0.4 s, `tapStrip`) or Enter opens/closes the menu (`expandedStrip`). Dragging, resizing, nudging, duplicating and Undo never open a menu. A tap that lands on the ✂ split button within 0.45 s of the first tap counts as the double-tap (it used to split the strip). Text, stickers, effects, captions, video layers and audio can be dragged up or down (more than 14 px): over the middle of a row of the same `type` it joins that row (`lane`, highlighted `.drop-into`), between rows / above / below it gets a new layer (`lane = uid()`, pink "New layer" line), moved in the array next to its new row (array order = layer order). Rows exist only for their strips, so a row left empty goes away. A pink note at the top of the screen, away from the timeline (`stripTip`), explains this for 9 s the first 3 times the editor is used, only once the editor is showing with strips on the timeline (`editorWithStrips()`: page loaded, start screen closed; it goes away if the start screen opens) (✕ ends it); there's also a Quick tip.
- **Main clips ↔ video layers** (`clipToLayer`/`layerToClip`, end of `timeline-strips.js`): a main video clip dragged up or down off the main row becomes a video layer (picture in picture, `scale` 100 so it looks the same) at the same time and part of the file (`mainDrop`: over the middle of a video-layer row it joins that row, else a new layer, line labelled "New video layer"); a video layer dropped on the main row goes back into the main video at its time (or the first free spot after it). Other main clips keep their places (offsets fixed first), so a gap is left for its 🗑. The layer gets its own `<video>` for the clip's file, loaded directly (not `loadTrackMedia`, which rejects phone recordings whose length isn't known until later); if it still fails, Undo rolls everything back and nothing is left behind. A drag only switches to up/down after 14 px with a mouse, 34 px by touch (44 px for main clips), and only when it's mostly vertical, so a sideways drag on a phone doesn't jump tracks. The strip note's counter key is `tapshop-strip-tip2` (reset once because it had been used up on the start screen).
- **🗑 Empty space** (`addGapBins`/`closeGap`, end of `timeline-strips.js`): every row shows a small dashed 🗑 in each gap of 0.15 s or more between two of its strips. On the main video row it deletes that time from the whole video (`rippleGap(A, d, true)`: everything after moves left; with `keep`, things inside the gap move to its start instead of being removed); on other rows only that row's later strips move. Dragging a strip over the middle of a row of another kind makes a new track above or below it (by which half).
- **Ripple delete** (end of `timeline-strips.js`, `rippleGap(A, d)`): deleting a main-row clip closes the gap and moves everything on the other rows with it: later main clips with their own `offset`, graphic tracks (text, stickers, effects; captions also drop and shift their `words`), and extra tracks (video layers, audio, music via `start`/`end`/`speed`). Items only on the deleted clip are removed, items crossing it get shorter. When there are captions, the toast points to **🔄 Redo captions** (`capRedo(id)` in `video-captions.js`, two `.cap-redo` buttons in the Captions strip: one at the top next to ★ Save my style and Delete captions, one in the Timing row): it re-listens to the timeline as it is now (cloud AI first if on and not turned off in the B-roll builder, else `listenAll` on the device), drops um/uh, and replaces the Captions track keeping its style; Undo-able. The snapshot taken before the delete covers it all, so one Undo restores everything.
- The first time the editor shows (start screen closed), the timeline toolbar glides to its far end and back (`video-toolbar.js`, scroll-snap off during the glide). Skipped when it all fits, for reduced motion, and stopped by any touch or wheel on the bar.

- AI captions and the B-roll builder's clip sorting load transformers.js from jsDelivr and models from Hugging Face in the visitor's browser, so don't add a Content-Security-Policy that blocks those.
- Chrome on Android lets idle videos "sleep" (`readyState` < 2, so `draw()` skipped them and the preview went black). `draw()` calls `wakeVideo(v, t)` for a sleeping video while paused: a short muted `play()`, then back to the frame and a redraw (`v._waking` guards it).
- Anything drawn from a `<video>` goes through `paintVideo`, and anything that changes the sound must be added to `mixSound` (`video-export.js`), so preview and export match.
- **Save video** (top bar) opens the export: an MP4 with H.264 picture and AAC sound at 30 fps, short side = the chosen resolution (`exportSize()`, so 1080p vertical is 1080×1920), the format TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube all accept. The frame-by-frame path (`video-export.js`) only uses `avc` + `aac`; otherwise the real-time recorder runs and, if its file isn't H.264 + AAC MP4 (`socialReady()`), says some apps may refuse it.
