Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. TapShopLite is free during early access, with no card and no trial clock. We'll tell you well before a full version arrives.

Why do I need an account?

So we can let you know about new features and the full version. We only ask for an email and a password, nothing else.

Where do my videos go?

Nowhere. Your clips are edited right on your phone or computer, and the finished video saves straight to your device.

Can I use the music on TikTok and YouTube?

Yes. The free music is made fresh on your device for your video, so there's no song library and no licence to buy. Songs you upload yourself need your own rights.

Do I need editing skills?

No. The video makers build the whole video for you. The editor is there if you want to fine-tune anything.

What can I use it on?

Phones, tablets, laptops and desktops: iPhone, Android, Mac and Windows, in Chrome, Safari or Edge.
