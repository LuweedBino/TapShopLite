Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026 (cloud AI added)

TapShopLite is a free video maker that runs in your web browser. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect and what we don't.

What we collect

Your email address, when you create an account (by email link, or by signing in with Google or another provider). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us; we only use the email address.

What we don't collect

Your videos and photos never leave your device. Editing, captions and the AI that sorts your clips run inside your browser. Nothing you edit is stored by us.

One exception you control: cloud AI in the B-roll Video Maker. When the “Use cloud AI” box is ticked (it is on unless you turn it off), the sound only of your talking clips is sent through our server to Groq, an AI company, to be written down as words. The words go to Groq's text AI, which marks fumbles and retakes. We don't keep the sound or the words. Groq handles them under its own privacy policy. Untick the box to keep everything on your device.

A daily count. To keep cloud AI free, we count how many times you use it each day. The count is kept with your account.

The first time you use captions or clip sorting, your browser downloads free AI models from Hugging Face and the jsDelivr network. They see a normal download request, not your videos.

How we use your email

To sign you in, to send the emails your account needs (confirming your email, resetting your password), and to tell you about important changes to TapShopLite, such as the full version arriving. Account emails are sent for us by Resend, an email service. We don't sell or rent your email, and we don't send it to advertisers.

Where it's kept

Accounts are stored with Supabase, our sign-in provider. Your browser also keeps your recent videos and settings on your own device; you can clear them any time from your browser settings.

Deleting your account

Ask us to delete your account and we'll remove your email and account details.

Children

TapShopLite is not meant for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any email from TapShopLite.
